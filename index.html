Our Story

The Journey Home

From 11,000 kilometers apart to the beginning of our life together.

What began with a glance across continents in September 2024 became something neither of us could have planned. We fell in love across time zones, meeting every two months, counting down the days, and carrying each other across oceans.

On July 12th, 2025, surrounded by family in India, we said our vows. Then came the waiting, the paperwork, the calls that stretched past midnight, the airports, and the goodbyes.

But love is patient. And now, finally, Chetna is home.

This evening is our way of celebrating everything at once — one beautiful year of marriage, Chetna's arrival in Canada, and the beginning of our life together here at Home. We are so grateful you are here to witness it.
